|---|---|---|---|

No residency clause found (to-confirm) | https://uwaterloo.ca/management-science-engineering/graduate-studies/future-students/programs ; .../future-students/faq |
| ECE PhD | **Explicitly allowed** | On-campus delivery. No residency clause found (to-confirm) | https://uwaterloo.ca/electrical-computer-engineering/doctor-philosophy-phd |
| Stratford School | No PhD of its own; faculty supervise through SYDE, CS, English, etc. | n/a | Games Institute students page |
