## R2.4 Co-supervision (G+PS policy, https://www.grad.ubc.ca/policies/supervision)
- A doctoral committee may have 2 co-supervisors plus at least 1 member. At least half must be G+PS members. A member from another program is *recommended*.
- CS faculty are G+PS members, so a CS professor can co-supervise an HDLC/MERM student under the general policy. Each program's own approval still applies (To-confirm with HDLC/MERM). UBCO faculty need non-G+PS approval.
- I found no stated restriction tied to part-time status.

## R2.5 EdD
- **EdD Educational Leadership & Policy (EDST)**: designed for working professionals. It uses cohorts of 10-15, summer sessions plus winter weekends, in person in Vancouver, with a 6-year limit. Tuition is market-priced (higher than the PhD). The dissertation is based on the student's workplace. Sources: https://edst.educ.ubc.ca/programs/edd-in-educational-leadership-and-policy/ and https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/doctor-of-education-educational-leadership-policy
- Fit caveat: the program targets education leaders (K-12, post-secondary, training directors). A corporate L&D / AI-transformation lead *might* qualify (inference). The topic is policy and leadership, not HCI.
