# Procurement Policy (v3, effective 2026-09-01)

## 1. Approval thresholds
- Purchases **up to USD 10,000**: approved by the requesting team's manager or the CTO.
- Purchases **above USD 10,000**: approved by the **Finance Director**. The CTO may not approve purchases above USD 10,000.
- If the approver is on leave, their delegate listed in the org chart approves on their behalf.

## 2. Required information before approval
An approver will only approve a request that states:
1. the **budget code** that will be charged (issued by Finance for the requesting team, current fiscal quarter), and
2. for any software that **sends company code or data to an external service**, a completed **security review** and the name of the reviewer.

Requests missing either item are returned to the requester.

## 3. Security reviews
Security reviews are performed by the engineering **Security Champion** for the current quarter (see org chart). Former Security Champions may not sign off reviews.

## 4. Budget codes
Budget codes change every fiscal quarter. Codes from previous quarters are closed and will be rejected by the payment system.
