# Procurement policy — effective 2026-10-01

1. Current team requirements are maintained by the team's research workload owner; engineering implementation owners do not maintain the current roster.

2. External source-code-confidential services require a cross-team quality/risk review bound to the current requirements revision and deployment controls.

3. Annual commitments up to and including USD 10000 are within technical leadership scope. Commitments above USD 10000 belong to Finance Director scope.

4. A purchase authorization must reference a completed risk review and the exact annual amount, current budget and controls.

For SecureScan Pro, EU-only processing and zero-retention are available contractual controls. Prices are annual USD; taxes and discounts are zero. The procurement working directory lists relevant staff for this request. Research workload records can change after a trial. A trial estimate is not a current roster.
